Excel Sales Data Analysis Project

Project Overview

This project involved analyzing sales transaction data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and understand sales distribution across countries. I also used XLOOKUP to match customer records across two datasets and identify unmatched records.

Objectives

- Analyze total sales performance.
- Identify the country with the highest sales.
- Determine the products generating the highest revenue.
- Identify the product with the highest quantity sold.
- Match customer records across two datasets using XLOOKUP.
- Identify unmatched records and provide recommendations based on the findings.

Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- XLOOKUP
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Data analysis and visualization

Key Findings

1. Total Sales

The total sales recorded in the dataset amounted to 103,106.79.

2. Sales by Country

The United Kingdom recorded the highest sales, amounting to 86,048.33.

3. Product Generating the Highest Revenue

Vintage Union Jack Doorstep generated the highest product sales revenue, amounting to 3,873.98.

4. Product with the Highest Quantity Sold

Assorted Farmyard Animals in Buckets recorded the highest quantity sold, with 3,312 units.

5. Customer Record Matching

I used XLOOKUP to match customer records across two datasets. The analysis identified five unmatched customer records, which require further investigation.

Recommendations

- Investigate the five unmatched customer records to determine the cause of the discrepancies.
- Monitor sales performance across countries to identify opportunities for growth.
- Maintain adequate stock of high-performing products to meet customer demand.
- Continue monitoring product revenue and sales quantities to support inventory planning and business decisions.
